🔹 Encapsulation in Java

Encapsulation means wrapping data (variables) and methods (functions) 
together into a single unit (class)** and restricting direct access to the data.

It is achieved by:
 Making variables private
 Providing public getter and setter methods

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔹 Why do we use Encapsulation?

* To protect data from unauthorized access
* To maintain data security
* To achieve better control over variables

---------------------------------------------------------------------------


🔹 Short Interview Answer:

> Encapsulation is the process of binding data and methods into a 
single unit (class) and restricting direct access using private variables 
with public getters and setters. It helps in data hiding and security.
